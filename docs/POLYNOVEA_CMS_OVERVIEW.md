# Why Organisations Choose Polynovea CMS

Polynovea CMS is for organisations that want control over their content operations without becoming dependent on a software vendor or building an entire CMS internally.

## The reason to choose it

Most CMS products are excellent at editing pages but weak at managing the business process behind them. They make it hard to model organisation-specific information, govern who can change it, understand dependencies, or adapt the system when the business changes.

Polynovea CMS is designed to give teams both flexibility and control: business users can work with structured content and clear workflows, while technical teams retain ownership of the code, data and deployment.

## Why open source matters

- **You own the system.** Your content model, data, deployment and integrations are not trapped behind a vendor’s pricing, roadmap or proprietary export format.
- **You can adapt it.** The CMS can be extended for your own products, markets, approval rules and internal processes instead of forcing the business into a generic template.
- **You can verify it.** Security, permissions and data handling are inspectable rather than black-box promises.
- **You have options.** Polynovea’s reference stack is Vercel, Supabase and Cloudflare R2, while the core data model is designed around PostgreSQL rather than a closed platform.

Open source does not mean “free and unsupported.” It means an organisation can choose its operating model: run it itself, work with a partner, or maintain it internally without losing control of the asset.

## Why not a basic CMS or a custom build?

| If you choose... | The trade-off | Polynovea CMS approach |
|---|---|---|
| A page builder | Fast initially, but business data and governance tend to live outside it. | Treat content, structured records, workflow and publishing as one operating system. |
| A proprietary enterprise CMS | Strong features, but vendor lock-in, licence cost and roadmap dependence. | Keep the source, data model and deployment under organisational control. |
| A bespoke internal CMS | Exact fit, but expensive to build and maintain from scratch. | Start from a governed, extensible foundation and invest engineering effort only where the business is genuinely different. |

## What the organisation gains

Teams can define the records they need — products, campaigns, market pages, case studies, editorial content or knowledge — and run them through a visible process. Drafts, versions, review ownership, comments, approvals, schedules and audit history reduce reliance on informal chat and individual memory.

The result is not just a better editor. It is a more reliable way to operate content at scale: faster change, fewer publishing mistakes, clearer accountability, and a platform the organisation can evolve on its own terms.

## Best fit

Polynovea CMS is a strong fit for growing or complex organisations that need more than a marketing-site editor: multi-team content operations, product information, regulated or approval-led publishing, multi-market content, or an internal platform that must remain adaptable over time.

## Product maturity note

The core schema, structured-record, workflow and collaboration foundations are in active development. The project’s public roadmap should be used to assess the status of specific advanced capabilities before adopting it for a production-critical use case.

## Product UI design system

The canonical product UI specification is maintained in `docs/design/POLYNOVEA_CMS_DESIGN_SYSTEM_V1.md`. New or materially redesigned CMS interfaces should use that document as the source of truth for theme tokens, semantic states, typography, surfaces, interaction hierarchy, accessibility and dark/light-theme portability.
